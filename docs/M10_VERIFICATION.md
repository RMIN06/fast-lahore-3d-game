## Checks run

`npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` (194 tests), `npm run validate:campus` and `npm run build` pass. The **full** Playwright suite (`npm run test:e2e`, 33 tests across all milestones) passes. After the last source change, 29 passed in one full run and the other 4 (smoke and interiors F, D, C) on a rerun, once two screenshot polls were given 20 s instead of 5 s (see below).

## Performance

- **Furniture streams with the interiors.** It is one instanced mesh per building and kind, shown only while that block's interior is active, plus the outdoor fixtures. The first version drew every block's furniture from anywhere: 316k triangles at Gate-5 instead of about 33k, dropping software-rendered FPS from about 20 to 3. With streaming it is about 64k there.
- **Interior budget with furniture** (`tests/interiors.spec.ts`, worst room per block): F 98 calls / 177k triangles, D 99 / 262k, C 82 / 132k, B 95 / 132k, A 84 / 52k, E 75 / 44k. All are within 250 calls and 800k triangles.
- **Frame rate.** The test machine was much slower during the final runs. The M9 commit itself measured 3.7 FPS at the department spawn, where M10 measured 3.0 (the difference is the in-building furniture). Two checks that wait for the picture to change after the Confidence-colours toggle timed out at 5 s, so they now wait up to 20 s. What they check is unchanged.

## Known limits

- Fixture layouts, the timetable, office hours and event days are placeholders (OPEN_QUESTIONS #18, #48, #49).
- You can walk through furniture: there are no colliders until the M13 props pass.
- Gates stay closed to the player (M4). The Masjid is reachable only at Friday Jummah, and Fresh Friez not at all (OPEN_QUESTIONS #50).
- The court and ground mini-games, and full mini-game versions of the book hunt, lab puzzle and quiz, come in M11.
- Friends wait at the meeting spot with no deadline.
